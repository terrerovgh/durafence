# Durafence.net catalog research

Researched 2026-09-25 for Durafencemetal. Primary evidence: public product pages and their live HTML form options. This is a factual catalog inventory, not a transfer of the source business's identity, policies, prices, inventory counts, warranties, or approvals. Durafencemetal's existing estimate section remains its pricing authority.

## Coverage

Reviewed [home](https://durafence.net/), [shop page 1](https://durafence.net/shop/), [shop page 2](https://durafence.net/shop/page/2/), [gates](https://durafence.net/product-category/dura-fence-gates/), [parts](https://durafence.net/product-category/dura-fence-parts/), and all 13 distinct product pages. Page 2 contains the 2-inch screw product; category counts and unique shop products agree: one fence system, three gates, eight parts (including planks and sample), one engineering offering. Web extraction failed for shop page 2, but a direct HTML download succeeded and confirmed the final product.

Image provenance is recorded separately in [durafence-images.json](./durafence-images.json). Examples show source products and must not be presented as Durafencemetal completed installations.

## Fence system

[Fence by linear foot](https://durafence.net/product/dura-fence-by-lineal-feet/): heights 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8 ft. Live attributes offer White, Bronze, and Wood; description mentions only the first two. Frame options are unpainted, white, and bronze. The source lists posts, rails, planks, small and large screws, and post caps as system components. It describes steel construction. Its form requires at least 10 ft; that is a source ordering constraint, not a Durafencemetal minimum. Attached gate selectors currently expose only 6-ft-high options. Do not infer that planks, posts, and rails all share the same alloy or thickness from general homepage copy.

## Planks and parts

### Planks

[Planks](https://durafence.net/product/dura-fence-planks/): 6-inch width; lengths/heights 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8 ft. Every height's live form exposes White, Bronze, and Wood Pattern, although the prose only mentions white/bronze. Wood Pattern is an appearance, not evidence that the plank is timber. Individual plank page does not establish alloy or gauge. Product care guidance is mild detergent and water plus periodic fastener checks. Avoid translating generic resistance claims into a warranty or absolute rust-proof claim.

### Posts

[Posts](https://durafence.net/product/dura-fence-post/): live form offers 2.5 × 2.5 in square × 8 ft long, in white, bronze, or unpainted. Prose also lists 3 × 3 in × 8 ft and says a cap accompanies the post. Its introductory claim of three sizes is inconsistent with the two listed and one selectable. Public catalog should prioritize the selectable size and invite confirmation of other sizes. No verified wall thickness or alloy is supplied.

### Post caps

[Caps](https://durafence.net/product/dura-fence-post-caps/): live size is 2.5 × 2.5 in; colors white, bronze, black. Additional-information attributes mention 2 × 2, 2.5 × 2.5, and 3 × 3 in. Description incorrectly appends an 8-ft length to cap sizes, evidently reusing post dimensions. Do not publish an 8-ft cap. Intended function is to cover the open post head. Material and fastening method are not verified by a technical drawing.

### Rails

[Rails](https://durafence.net/product/dura-fence-rails/): 24-ft stock length, rectangular 1 × 2 in profile, wall 0.125 in. Published inner dimensions are 0.75 × 1.75 (units omitted in those table cells; inches implied by matching outer dimensions). Weight is listed as 0.81 lb/linear ft. Live finish options: unpainted, bronze, white. The generic additional-information weight of 100 kg conflicts with the dimensional table and should not be used. A 24-ft stock length is not a supported 24-ft clear span; source prose about reducing posts is not an engineering design. Alloy and permissible post spacing are not substantiated.

### Fasteners

[5/16 screws](https://durafence.net/product/dura-fence-self-tapping-screws-5-16/): boxes of 100; nominal catalog name 5/16. Detailed prose says 5/16-in diameter and 2-in length, while the short description simply repeats the other 2-in product. It describes coated steel. Preserve the nominal name and quantity; confirm actual diameter, drive size, and length before representing an exact technical specification or assigning it to a particular joint.

[2-inch screws](https://durafence.net/product/durafence-self-tapping-screws-2%E2%80%B3/): self-tapping screws, nominal 2-in length, 100 per box. Source describes steel with a corrosion-resistant coating. No independently specified diameter, drive, coating designation, shear rating, or connection schedule is available. Do not treat either screw as interchangeable solely because the source repeats dimensions.

### Touch-up spray

[Paint spray](https://durafence.net/product/dura-fence-paint-spray/): live options white and bronze; suitable catalog role is finish touch-ups. Source gives broad outdoor/coating claims, but no verified can volume, specific chemistry, coverage rate, or numeric drying time. Do not invent those details or guarantee universal substrate compatibility.

### Sample box

[Sample](https://durafence.net/product/dura-fence-sample/): demonstration pieces approximately 10–12 inches for comparing appearance and texture. Source shipping/reimbursement policies are business-specific and the page's advertised price and shipping prose are inconsistent. Neither policy nor price should become Durafencemetal's. The generic 100-kg metadata is not credible sample weight.

## Engineering / NOA

[Engineering drawings](https://durafence.net/product/durafence-noa-engineering-drawings/): source markets a project-specific documentation package with engineering drawings, layouts/elevations, foundations, anchorage/connections, material schedule and applicable calculations; PDF and optional DWG are described. It does not display a verifiable NOA approval number or approval expiration in the product text reviewed. This establishes a documentation category, not that every Durafencemetal configuration is certified, permitted, hurricane-rated, or automatically accepted. Its engineer service, processing time, reimbursement and municipal-review support cannot be promised on Durafencemetal's behalf. Use inquiry wording for project documentation and confirm scope before quoting.

## Editorial decisions for the implementation

- Organize the same products into fence systems, three access types, planks, structural parts, finishing/fastening parts, samples, and documentation.
- Use new English product descriptions built around the facts above, with dimensions and useful selection guidance. Avoid reproducing source promotional paragraphs.
- Clearly distinguish currently selectable variants from additional dimensions described in source prose. Link all buying/price calls to the existing Durafencemetal estimate workflow.
- Do not import source prices, minimum orders, stock totals, ratings, corporate history, phone numbers, guarantees, engineering approval claims, delivery times, or customer/project statistics.
- Homepage references 24 gauge and American production in general promotional text. Product-specific evidence is insufficient to apply those claims to every part or to Durafencemetal's operations. Existing independently established Durafencemetal claims require their own evidence.

## Research limits

This review observes what the supplier publishes; it is not a dimensional inspection, approval-database validation, or manufacturer datasheet. Several product descriptions conflict with form selections and include repeated generic metadata. Record those conflicts rather than silently merging all values into guaranteed stock. No checkout or paid engineering purchase was performed.
